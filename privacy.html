Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The Story Garden ("we", "us") designs sculptural hydroponic growing systems. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit mystorygardens.com, contact us, or place an order, and how we use it. We keep this simple: we collect only what we need, we don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

What we collect

When you contact us

If you send us a message through our contact form, we receive the information you enter:

  • your name and email address;
  • the type of inquiry you choose;
  • if you fill them in, your company name, an estimated quantity and a preferred date;
  • your message.

When you place an order

Pre-orders are paid on a secure checkout page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment card details directly; we never see or store your full card number. We receive the details needed to fulfill your order, such as your name, email address, shipping address, the items you ordered and the payment status.

When you browse the site

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, the services that deliver it automatically receive basic technical information when pages load, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. See "Services we use" below.

How we use your information

  • to reply to your questions and wholesale or partnership inquiries;
  • to process, ship and support your order, including order updates and help with returns;
  • to keep records we're required to keep, such as for taxes and accounting;
  • to protect our website and customers from fraud and abuse.

We don't send marketing emails unless you ask us to, and we never sell or rent your personal information.

Services we use

We rely on a few trusted companies to run the website. Each handles your information under its own privacy policy:

  • Formspree receives contact-form messages and forwards them to us by email. It stores submissions and filters spam. Formspree privacy policy
  • Stripe processes payments and runs our checkout page. Stripe privacy policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log technical information such as IP addresses for security. GitHub privacy statement
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this site, so your browser connects to Google to load them. Google privacy policy

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages for as long as we need them to answer you and follow up, and order records for as long as needed to fulfill your order and meet legal and tax requirements. When we no longer need information, we delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. To make a request, email us at info@mystorygardens.com. We'll respond within 30 days and won't treat you differently for making one.

Children

Our website and products are intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and payments are handled by Stripe. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at info@mystorygardens.com.
